Collection inventory and recovery limits

Counts appear after preparation or verification, including empty collections.

Scope: all 18 known app root collections, including bill revisions, revision approvals and year-close events, with original document IDs and Firestore data types. Unknown collections/subcollections, Firebase Auth login accounts/passwords, uploaded Storage files, rules and hosting assets are not part of this JSON.

This is a two-pass content check, not an atomic point-in-time snapshot. Keep all staff writes paused. Automatic cloud backups and point-in-time recovery require paid Firebase services and are not enabled by this workflow. Login accounts need a separate owner-controlled Auth export.

Free Firestore quota is shared by the whole app. Check usage before repeating a large export. The read allowance stops an oversized preparation without presenting a partial file as a complete backup.

Restore is deliberately not a browser button. Verify first, rehearse recovery in an isolated environment and check record counts/balances before any owner-approved production recovery. Checksums detect corruption; they do not prove who created a file.

Keep at least one older verified copy. Never save recovery files inside the app's source folder or commit them to Git.
